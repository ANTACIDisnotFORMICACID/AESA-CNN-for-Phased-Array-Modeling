# V2.18 preregistration

## Scope and seal

- Array: 4x4 only.
- Training directions: V2.17 canonical a4x4 train split (45,071).
- Validation directions: V2.17 canonical a4x4 validation split (9,780).
- Training commands: the frozen 64 V2.16 commands, with amplitudes recomputed
  from the canonical complex operator.
- Validation commands: the frozen 512 V2.17 validation commands.
- Seeds: 21801, 21802, 21803.
- Confirmatory blind test: sealed and unavailable to all V2.18 tools.

## Fixed training contract

- 240 epochs, batch size 512, AdamW, weight decay `1e-5`.
- Learning rate `1e-3`, changed to `3e-4` at epoch 181.
- Checkpoint selection: validation amplitude linear NMSE only.
- V2.16 objective: amplitude linear NMSE + `0.1 * power NMSE` +
  `0.03 * dB Huber` + residual-energy regularization +
  `0.001 * separation`.
- The L=4 filtration SSH transform is fit on training directions only.

## Arms and stopping rules

Baseline arms are `control_v212_canonical`, `dcar_static_pm_canonical`, and
`dcar_gated_pm_canonical`. Their model implementations are constructed through
the frozen V2.16 builders. Baseline reproduction passes only when every gate in
the approved plan passes. A failed baseline stops the experiment at the
data/target adaptation layer.

Only after baseline reproduction passes may `dcar_gated_pm_opaux` be trained.
It is architecture-identical to `dcar_gated_pm_canonical`; its only change is
`0.1 *` solid-angle-weighted normalized complex Ephi operator NMSE. No Q loss
is used and the weight is not tuned on validation.

The possible public statuses are:

- `BASELINE_REPRODUCTION_PASS` or `BASELINE_REPRODUCTION_FAIL`
- `OPTIMIZATION_PASS` or `OPTIMIZATION_FAIL`
- `CONFIRMATION_NOT_OPENED` (until separately authorized confirmation)

No failed gate authorizes structure, weight, or threshold scanning.
