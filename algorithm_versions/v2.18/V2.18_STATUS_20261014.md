### Attribution result

- `MIGRATION_ATTRIBUTION_PASS`.
- The expected overlaps were verified without tracing the remaining 900 old
  validation directions: 3,200 canonical-train and 800 canonical-validation.
- Maximum direction error: `6.00685e-8`.
- The train-only canonical-to-V2.16 amplitude scale is `7.284856841351336`.
- On the 800 validation overlaps, aggregate amplitude NMSE is `-142.015 dB`,
  worst-command NMSE is `-140.413 dB`, and strong-point relative-error P99 is
  `4.54821e-7`; every data-equivalence gate passed.
- The command-shift signal did not pass: gated-minus-control advantage was
  `-0.344 dB` on P2 old16 and `-0.329 dB` on P3 frozen512.
- The optimization-instability signal passed. On P3, the V2.18 control
  cross-seed range was `0.132 dB`, while gated-PM was `1.445 dB`.
- Therefore Stage-2 authorizes exactly the B and D phase-aligned auxiliary
  arms. `CONFIRMATION_NOT_OPENED` remains active.

### Stage-2 final result — 2026-10-15
