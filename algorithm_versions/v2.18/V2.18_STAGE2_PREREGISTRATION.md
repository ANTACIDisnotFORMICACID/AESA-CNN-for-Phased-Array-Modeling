Date: 2026-10-14. This extension does not replace or reinterpret the frozen

## Order and stop rule

1. Run the train-only normalization and 800-direction equivalence gate.
2. If it passes, evaluate the frozen P0-P3 panels without opening blind data.
3. Phase-aligned auxiliary training is authorized only when either the P2-to-P3
   gated advantage drops by at least 0.30 dB, or the V2.18 gated P3 range exceeds
   the control range by at least 0.50 dB (equivalently its sample standard
   deviation exceeds control by at least 0.30 dB).
4. If authorized, train exactly the two new arms and three frozen seeds. No
   fifth arm, auxiliary-weight scan, epoch extension, or command augmentation is
   permitted.

The equivalence thresholds, arms, seeds, 240-epoch budget, phase-aligned loss
weight `0.1`, validation-only checkpoint selection, engineering gates, and
`CONFIRMATION_NOT_OPENED` boundary are exactly those in the approved Stage-2
plan. Raw complex NMSE is diagnostic only; Q and direction-wise phase-aligned
complex NMSE are the operator gates.

Version records and reports must separate: (1) implemented algorithm change,
(2) data/protocol change, and (3) measured evidence. V2.12 is the base model,
V2.13-V2.14 are audits/sampling studies, V2.15 is the graph-residual experiment,
V2.16 is the controlled DCAR model, V2.17 is canonical/cross-array preparation,
and V2.18 is the canonical transport and attribution experiment.
