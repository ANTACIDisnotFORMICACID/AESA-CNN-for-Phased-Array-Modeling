# V2.18 Canonical Migration Stage-2 results

Date: 2026-10-15 CST. Final status:

- `MIGRATION_ATTRIBUTION_PASS`
- `PHASE_AUX_EXPERIMENT_FAIL`
- `ENGINEERING_CANDIDATE_NONE`
- selected canonical baseline: arm A, `control_v212_canonical`
- `CONFIRMATION_NOT_OPENED`

This result does not alter the frozen Stage-1 `BASELINE_REPRODUCTION_FAIL` and
does not overturn the V2.16 result on its original protocol. It shows that the
V2.16 gated DCAR advantage did not migrate robustly enough to the canonical
4x4 protocol, and that the single preregistered phase-aligned auxiliary change
was insufficient to create an engineering candidate.

## 1. What changed and what did not

V2.18 Stage-2 did not add a new backbone. Arms A/C reuse the completed Stage-1
checkpoints. Arms B/D use exactly the same control/gated-PM architectures and
paired initial states, adding only `0.1 *` solid-angle-weighted,
direction-wise phase-aligned complex Ephi NMSE during training. Parameter
counts and inference graphs are unchanged.

The algorithm lineage remains:

- V2.12: amplitude-predictive complex rank-1 backbone with filtration SSH,
  Fourier residual and analytic propagation phase;
- V2.13-V2.14: audits and sampling studies, not promoted CNNs;
- V2.15: direction-conditioned local graph residual; evidence supported local
  message-passing bias, not true S-topology causality;
- V2.16: controlled row/column DCAR inside the V2.12-depth backbone;
- V2.17: canonical/cross-array data and independent feasibility pilots;
- V2.18: transport, attribution and one controlled auxiliary-supervision test.

## 2. Migration attribution

The adapter gate passed before any new training:

- matched 3,200 old-validation directions to canonical train and 800 to
  canonical validation; the remaining 900 were not traced;
- maximum direction error: `6.00685e-8`;
- train-only canonical-to-V2.16 amplitude scale: `7.284856841351336`;
- 800-direction aggregate amplitude NMSE: `-142.015 dB`;
- worst-command NMSE: `-140.413 dB`;
- strong-point relative-error P99: `4.54821e-7`.

Therefore the Stage-1 failure is not explained by an amplitude-target adapter
error. The P2 old16 and P3 frozen512 gated-minus-control advantages were
`-0.344 dB` and `-0.329 dB`; the command-shift signal did not pass. The
optimization-instability signal did pass: V2.18 P3 control had a `0.132 dB`
cross-seed range, versus `1.445 dB` for gated-PM.

## 3. Four-arm canonical validation result

All values below are three-seed means in dB; more-negative error is better.

| Arm | Solid-angle amplitude | Command-family macro | Phase-aligned operator | Q | Strong `[-3,0]` | Weak `<-20` | Deep null `<-30` |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| A control | -26.268 | -26.272 | -23.391 | -22.067 | -32.821 | -13.604 | -6.071 |
| B control + phase aux | -26.371 | -26.359 | -23.427 | -22.326 | -33.441 | -13.425 | -5.769 |
| C gated-PM | -25.939 | -25.944 | -23.096 | -21.862 | -33.011 | -12.844 | -5.306 |
| D gated-PM + phase aux | -26.293 | -26.287 | -23.398 | -22.423 | -33.712 | -13.643 | -6.253 |

Relative to A:

- B improved overall amplitude by only `0.103 dB`, phase-aligned operator by
  `0.035 dB`, and Q by `0.259 dB`. It improved strong field by `0.620 dB`, but
  weak and deep-null means degraded by `0.179/0.302 dB`; seed 21802 weak-field
  degradation was `0.930 dB`.
- C retained the Stage-1 canonical failure: overall amplitude degraded by
  `0.329 dB`, with weak/deep-null degradation of `0.759/0.765 dB` and high
  seed variance.
- D nearly matched A overall (`+0.025 dB` amplitude, `+0.014 dB` macro),
  improved Q by `0.356 dB`, strong field by `0.892 dB`, weak field by
  `0.039 dB`, and deep null by `0.182 dB`. These redistributions are useful
  diagnostics but do not satisfy the frozen overall/macro gates.

D versus C showed the intended mechanism direction: mean phase-aligned
operator, Q and amplitude gains were `0.301/0.561/0.355 dB`. Nevertheless the
mechanism gate failed because one weak-field seed degraded by `0.308 dB`, just
above the frozen `0.30 dB` limit. Thresholds were not relaxed post hoc.

All four arms selected their best checkpoint in the last 10% of the 240-epoch
budget and are marked `BUDGET_SENSITIVE`; the budget was not extended.

The auxiliary loss adds no inference parameters or MACs. Measured B/A and D/C
throughput deltas were `+2.9%` and `-1.6%`, within the 5% same-architecture
gate. D remained about 10% slower than B in this audit and failed the required
`0.50 dB` macro advantage; DCAR therefore does not justify its added execution
complexity on the canonical protocol.

## 5. Decision

- Do not promote B, C or D.
- Retain V2.16 gated-PM as the best completed design on the original V2.16
  protocol, without claiming canonical transfer.
- Retain A as the current robust canonical 4x4 baseline.
- Do not scan the auxiliary weight, add a fifth arm, extend epochs, or open the
  blind test under V2.18.
- Raw complex NMSE remains diagnostic only. Model claims use amplitude, Q and
  direction-wise phase-aligned complex metrics.
